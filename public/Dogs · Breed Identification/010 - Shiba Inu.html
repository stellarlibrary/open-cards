Shiba Inu

A compact Japanese spitz with small upright ears, a dense coat and a curled or sickle-shaped tail.

Look for

Look for triangular ears that lean slightly forward, a compact body and a high-carried tail. Red examples often have pale areas along the cheeks and underside.

Tell it apart

An Akita usually has a much larger, heavier build. A curled tail alone is common to many breeds.
