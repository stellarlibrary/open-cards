Beagle

A compact scent hound with drop ears and a tail usually held fairly high when alert.

Look for

Look at the legs and ears together: the dog stands well above the ground, and its rounded ears do not normally trail near the feet.

Tell it apart

A Basset Hound has shorter legs, more substantial bone and much longer ears. Tricolour coats occur in both.
