Greyhound

A large sighthound with a deep chest, long legs and a slender neck and head.

Look for

Look for a long, narrow muzzle, folded rose ears, a marked abdominal tuck and a tapering tail. This is the classic running silhouette.

Tell it apart

A Whippet shares these proportions in a smaller package. Without an object for scale, shape alone is often not decisive.
