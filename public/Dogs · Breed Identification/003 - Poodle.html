Poodle

A squarely built dog with a coat that naturally grows in close curls. Standard, Miniature and Toy varieties differ in size.

Look for

Look for the long, straight muzzle, hanging ears and dense curls. Hair clipped short on the face makes the head shape easier to see.

Tell it apart

A haircut is not a breed marker. A Bichon Frise is also curly, but typically has a shorter muzzle and a plume carried over the back.
