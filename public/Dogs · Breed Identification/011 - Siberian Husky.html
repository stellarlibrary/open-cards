Siberian Husky

A medium-sized sled dog with a balanced, comparatively light frame and a dense double coat.

Look for

Look at the upright triangular ears and the bushy tail hanging behind the legs. The tail may also lift in a loose curve, but it does not usually lie tightly curled on the back.

Tell it apart

An Alaskan Malamute generally has a broader head, heavier bone and ears set farther apart. Blue eyes can occur in Huskies, but are not required.
