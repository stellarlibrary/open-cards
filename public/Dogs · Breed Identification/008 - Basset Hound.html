Basset Hound

A heavy-boned scent hound built low to the ground, with very long, low-set ears.

Look for

The legs are short relative to the depth of the chest. Look for loose skin and ears that hang well below the jaw.

Tell it apart

A Beagle is taller on the legs with a lighter, more compact profile. A Dachshund is low too but has a narrower head and less pendulous ears.
