Border Collie

A medium-sized herding dog with a lean, ready-to-move build, seen in both rough and smooth coats.

Look for

The body is slightly longer than tall. Ears can be upright or partly tipped, and the tail tends to be carried low when the dog is relaxed.

Tell it apart

A rough-coated Australian Shepherd may look similar but often has a stockier outline. Merle and black-and-white are not decisive markers.
