Dalmatian

A smooth-coated, athletic dog with distinct round spots on a white background.

Look for

The short coat makes the individual black spots on the trunk and legs easy to see. Liver-brown spots are another recognized color.

Tell it apart

A ticked English Pointer can look spotted too. Ticking is usually many small flecks, often alongside large colour patches, rather than broadly scattered round spots.
