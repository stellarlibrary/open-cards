German Shepherd Dog

A strong, somewhat long-bodied herding dog with a wedge-shaped head and upright ears.

Look for

In profile, the upright ears frame the wedge-shaped head. The body is longer than it is tall, and the bushy tail hangs below the back.

Tell it apart

Color is unreliable. A Belgian Malinois is typically squarer in outline and lighter in build; compare body proportions before coat color.
