Alaskan Malamute

A powerful freighting sled dog with a broad head, heavy bone and a plumed tail.

Look for

Notice the substantial muzzle and relatively small triangular ears, set wide on the head. The heavy tail often arches over the back.

Tell it apart

A Siberian Husky typically has a lighter, more agile outline, with the ears placed higher and closer together.
