Basenji

A short-coated, finely built hound with upright ears, forehead wrinkles and a tightly curled tail.

Look for

The side view shows the slim legs, smooth coat, and the tail curled above the rump. Fine forehead wrinkles are easier to see from the front on an alert dog.

Tell it apart

A Shiba Inu also has upright ears and a curled tail, but its thicker double coat gives it a fuller outline.
