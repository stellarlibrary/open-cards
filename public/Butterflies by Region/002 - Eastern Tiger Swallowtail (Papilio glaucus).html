Eastern Tiger Swallowtail Papilio glaucus

Field marks

On the upper surface, bold black tiger-like bars run inward from the front edge of the yellow forewing, and each hindwing bears a narrow tail.

Eastern North America • Deciduous woodland edges and river corridors. Adults sometimes gather at damp ground to take up water and dissolved minerals.

Identification note

Males are always yellow, whereas females occur in two forms: a yellow morph with broader blue hindwing shading and a dark morph that mimics the distasteful Pipevine Swallowtail while keeping faint tiger stripes underneath. Farther north, the Canadian Tiger Swallowtail looks nearly identical.
