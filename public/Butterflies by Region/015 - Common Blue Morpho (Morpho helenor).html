Common Blue Morpho Morpho helenor

Field marks

With the wings folded, the brown underside displays a row of dark eyespots ringed with lighter color, hiding the brilliant blue upper surface.

Tropical Central and South America • Forest edges and trails. The bright upper surface and the camouflaged underside look very different.

Identification note

The famous metallic blue on the upperwings comes from light-reflecting microscopic structures on the scales, creating a bright flash in flight that vanishes the instant the butterfly lands and closes its wings. Several related Morpho species share similar eyespotted undersides and differ in the width of their dark upperwing borders.
