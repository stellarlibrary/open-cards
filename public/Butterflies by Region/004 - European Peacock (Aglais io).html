European Peacock Aglais io

Field marks

On the upper surface, a large multicolored eyespot sits toward the outer corner of each wing: two on the forewings and two on the hindwings, bold against the rusty-red ground color.

Europe and temperate Asia • Gardens, hedgerows, and woodland edges. The large markings show when the adult opens its wings.

Identification note

When the butterfly closes its wings to rest or hibernate over winter, the four eyespots vanish behind a charcoal-brown underside patterned like dead bark or a dry leaf. Flashing the open wings can startle foraging birds.
