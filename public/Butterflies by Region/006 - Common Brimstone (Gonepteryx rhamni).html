Common Brimstone Gonepteryx rhamni

Field marks

On the underside of the folded wings, pale green surfaces taper to pointed corners with raised leaf-like veins and a tiny brown-orange spot.

Europe into temperate Asia • Hedges and woodland rides, especially where buckthorns grow. Adults often rest with wings closed.

Identification note

In flight, upperwing color separates the sexes: males are vivid sulfur-yellow, whereas females are pale greenish-white and often mistaken for large white butterflies. In Mediterranean Europe, male Cleopatra butterflies look similar at rest but carry large orange flushes on the upper forewings.
