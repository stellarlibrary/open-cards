Blue Pansy Junonia oenone

Field marks

On the upper surface, one large blue patch lies on each hindwing, while conspicuous broken white bands sit farther forward on the dark forewings.

Sub-Saharan Africa • Sunny clearings, gardens, and open woodland. The adult shown has a particularly bold upperwing pattern.

Identification note

The blue patches are often smaller and duller on females, so check the pale bands on the dark forewings as well. At rest with closed wings, a cryptic brown underside hides the blue altogether.
