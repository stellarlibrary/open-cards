Banded Peacock Anartia fatima

Field marks

On the upper surface, find the cream bands on both wing pairs, then look just inside the hindwing band. A broken arc of red marks lies there, not on the forewing.

Mexico and Central America • Gardens, grassy clearings, and other disturbed sunny places.

Identification note

Pale bands vary from creamy white to yellowish and are not enough on their own; on a worn butterfly, confirm with any remaining hindwing red marks and the local range. Lookalike Adelpha sisters place orange patches near the forewing tips instead.
