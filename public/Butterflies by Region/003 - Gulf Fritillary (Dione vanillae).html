Gulf Fritillary Dione vanillae

Field marks

Many elongated, bright silver-white spots contrast with the rusty brown underside. Look for them when the adult folds its wings at a flower.

Southern United States, Mexico, and tropical America • Sunny places with passionflower vines. Also called Agraulis vanillae in some guides.
