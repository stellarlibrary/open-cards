Orange-tip Anthocharis cardamines

Field marks

On the upper surface, bright orange covers the outer tip of each white forewing beside a small dark discal spot. The orange patches are a male field mark; females have dark gray-black forewing tips instead.

Europe and temperate Asia • Spring meadows and hedgerows; adults frequent grassy openings. This adult is a male.

Identification note

Because females lack the orange patches, they are easily mistaken from above for Small or Green-veined Whites. Check the folded hindwing underside: both sexes of Orange-tip share distinctive mottled moss-green and white camouflage.
