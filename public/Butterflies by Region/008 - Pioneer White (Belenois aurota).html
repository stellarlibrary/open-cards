Pioneer White Belenois aurota

Field marks

On the underside of the folded wings, dark patches cluster near the tips and margins while thin dark lines trace inward along the veins, so the resting butterfly is not uniformly white.

Africa and parts of southern and western Asia • Open dry country and gardens. The butterfly shown is resting with its wings together.

Identification note

Also called the Brown-veined White in Africa, it varies noticeably by sex and season: females carry heavier dark borders and vein lines than males, and dry-season adults can look paler underneath than wet-season broods.
