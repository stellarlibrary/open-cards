Monarch Danaus plexippus

Field marks

On the upper surface, black veins divide the orange wings into panels, and rows of white dots sit inside the broad black border rather than in the middle of the orange panels.

North America • Meadows, gardens, and other open habitats with nectar plants. The caterpillar feeds on milkweeds.

Identification note

A Viceroy (Limenitis archippus) mimics this orange-and-black pattern in North America, but has an extra black cross-line arcing across each hindwing perpendicular to the veins. Male Monarchs also carry a small black scent spot along a central vein on each hindwing that females lack.
