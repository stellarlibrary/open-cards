Australian Painted Lady Vanessa kershawi

Field marks

On the upper surface, small pale spots punctuate the dark forewing tips, while a separate row of dark, blue-centered eyespots runs along the outer orange hindwings.

Australia • Sunny grasslands and gardens. Adults often spread their wings to bask or feed.

Identification note

The cosmopolitan Painted Lady (Vanessa cardui) looks very similar but is slightly larger and typically lacks bright blue centers in its black hindwing spots. With wings closed at rest, the orange upper surface disappears behind cryptic brown-and-white marbling.
