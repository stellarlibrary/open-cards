Red Postman Heliconius erato

Field marks

Each forewing has a red cross-band; the much paler hindwing bands are separate. A similar red pattern occurs in other Heliconius species, so the band alone is not proof of species.

Neotropical forest edges and clearings • Wing patterns vary across the range and are shared by some co-mimics.
