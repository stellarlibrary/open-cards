Symmetry of the normal curve

At equal distances from the normal mean, the lower tail on one side equals the upper tail on the other side. Total area under the curve is 1.

In use

Z is standard normal and P(Z≤1)≈0.8413.

About 0.3413. The area from −1 to 0 equals that from 0 to 1, which is 0.8413−0.5. Single endpoints have probability zero.
