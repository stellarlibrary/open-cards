Snow Gum Eucalyptus pauciflora

Field marks

The adult leaves are elongated and several main veins run roughly lengthwise. The smooth bark sheds in patches or ribbons, uncovering surfaces of different ages and colors.

Southeastern Australia • Common in colder uplands, including some alpine treeline woods.

Identification note

Whereas most eucalypts send side veins outward at an angle from a central midrib, Snow Gum's main veins run nearly parallel down the thick adult blade—a steady check when bark colors shift with weather and season. At windswept subalpine treelines, trees often grow in twisted, multi-stemmed clumps.
