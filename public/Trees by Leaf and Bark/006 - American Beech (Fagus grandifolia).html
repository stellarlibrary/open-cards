American Beech Fagus grandifolia

Field marks

The side veins run nearly parallel and reach the pointed teeth along the leaf edge. The trunk is usually smooth and ash gray rather than deeply furrowed.

Eastern North America • Shaded woods. Some dry leaves persist on branches into winter.

Identification note

Even in winter when pale, papery dead leaves cling to lower twigs, Beech stands out by its smooth gray trunk and long, narrow, sharp-pointed copper buds. Understory American Hornbeam (Carpinus caroliniana) also has smooth gray bark, but its trunk ripples with muscle-like flutes and its leaves are doubly toothed.
