American Sweetgum Liquidambar styraciflua

Field marks

Five narrow-pointed lobes radiate from near the leaf stalk. The gray-brown bark has narrow, wavy ridges separated by furrows. The sharply pointed star outline is the stronger clue.

Southeastern and eastern North America into parts of Central America • Bottomlands and mixed hardwood woods.

Identification note

Because its five-pointed leaves turn vivid red, yellow, and purple in autumn, Sweetgum is often mistaken for a maple—but Sweetgum leaves grow alternately along the twig rather than in opposite pairs. Corky ridges on twigs and spiky woody seed balls ('gumballs') confirm the ID year-round.
