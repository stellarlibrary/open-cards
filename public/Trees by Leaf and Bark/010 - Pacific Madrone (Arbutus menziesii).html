Pacific Madrone Arbutus menziesii

Field marks

The leaves are thick, smooth-looking, and oval. The thin copper to reddish outer bark curls and peels, exposing a smoother, paler surface beneath.

Pacific North America • Coastal and foothill woodlands, often with oaks or conifers on well-drained ground.
