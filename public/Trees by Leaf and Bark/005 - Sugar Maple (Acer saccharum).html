Sugar Maple Acer saccharum

Field marks

The large sinuses between the main pointed lobes are broadly U-shaped. On mature trunks the gray-brown bark breaks into long, irregular ridges and plates.

Eastern North America • Cool mixed hardwood forest. Leaf color changes with season; the outline and lobe gaps are more useful.

Identification note

Like other maples, its leaves and twigs grow in opposite pairs. Red Maple leaves have sharper, V-shaped sinuses and finely serrated lobe edges rather than Sugar Maple's smooth-margined U-shaped gaps, while Norway Maple exudes milky white sap from a freshly snapped leaf stalk.
