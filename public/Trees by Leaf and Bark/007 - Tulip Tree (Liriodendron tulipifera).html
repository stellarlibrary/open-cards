Tulip Tree Liriodendron tulipifera

Field marks

The broad leaf ends in a shallow central notch between two angular lobes, giving the tip a squared-off look. The bark on a mature trunk has interlacing ridges and furrows.

Eastern North America • Tall trees of moist, well-drained woods. Also called tulip poplar, though it is not a true poplar.

Identification note

Despite the nickname 'tulip poplar' or 'yellow poplar,' it belongs to the magnolia family rather than the true poplars (Populus). Sapling bark is smooth greenish-gray with pale vertical streaks before developing deep furrows, and winter twigs end in flat, two-scaled 'duck-bill' buds.
