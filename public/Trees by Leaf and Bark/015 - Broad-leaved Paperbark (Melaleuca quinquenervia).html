Broad-leaved Paperbark Melaleuca quinquenervia

Field marks

Several prominent veins run lengthwise on the firm blade. Thick, pale bark peels in stacked, papery layers, not a few isolated rigid plates.

Eastern Australia and nearby parts of the southwest Pacific • Native in wetlands; invasive in parts of Florida.

Identification note

The species name quinquenervia refers to the five (occasionally three to seven) longitudinal veins running from base to tip of the aromatic leaf. Unlike eucalypts that shed smooth ribbons to leave a hard trunk, Paperbark builds thick, spongy sheets of pale bark and bears creamy 'bottlebrush' flower spikes.
