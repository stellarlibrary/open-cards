Broad-leaved Paperbark Melaleuca quinquenervia

Field marks

Several prominent veins run lengthwise on the firm blade. Thick, pale bark peels in stacked, papery layers, not a few isolated rigid plates.

Eastern Australia and nearby parts of the southwest Pacific • Native in wetlands; invasive in parts of Florida.
