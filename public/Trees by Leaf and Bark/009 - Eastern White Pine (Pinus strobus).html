Eastern White Pine Pinus strobus

Field marks

The needles are long, slim, and flexible, with five in each intact bundle. Mature trunk bark develops rough gray-brown ridges and furrows.

Eastern North America • A conifer of mixed woods. Look at the bases of intact needle bundles, not the number of needles in a whole twig.
