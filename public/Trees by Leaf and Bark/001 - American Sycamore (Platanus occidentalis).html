American Sycamore Platanus occidentalis

Field marks

The broad leaf has palmately arranged, pointed lobes with prominent teeth. On the trunk, bark sheds in irregular plates to leave a pale green, gray, and cream mosaic, unlike typical maple bark.

Eastern and central North America • Often a large riverbank or floodplain tree. Check the upper trunk as well as the darker base.
