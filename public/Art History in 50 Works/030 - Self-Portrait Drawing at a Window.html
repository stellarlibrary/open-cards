Self-Portrait Drawing at a Window

Rembrandt van Rijn, 1648 • Etching, drypoint, and engraving • Cleveland Museum of Art.

Look closely

Lightly worked paper at left evokes daylight entering through the window, while dense webs of cross-hatched etching and drypoint build the shadowed room at right. Across that tonal gradient, Rembrandt brings sharp focus to his own steady gaze and working hands.
