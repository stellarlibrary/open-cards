Green Tara

Central Tibet, ca. 1260s • Gum tempera, ink, and gold on cotton • Thangka attributed to Aniko • Cleveland Museum of Art.

Look closely

Green Tara’s central figure is painted at a much larger scale than the surrounding deities and stands out vividly against the red throne back immediately behind her. That pairing of dominant scale and complementary color anchors devotional focus on the compassionate protector.
