Goryeo Celadon Vase with Clouds and Cranes

Korea, Goryeo dynasty, 1300s • Celadon with inlaid white and black slip • Cleveland Museum of Art.

Look closely

They sit within the surface. The potter cut the design into the clay, filled the recesses with contrasting slips, and glazed over the whole vessel, giving the cranes and clouds a muted contrast against the green.
