Night-Shining White

Han Gan, Tang China, ca. 750 • Ink on paper handscroll • The Met. Painted portrait of Emperor Xuanzong’s prized charger, surrounded by centuries of collectors’ seals and colophons.

Look closely

Night-Shining White’s lifted foreleg, wide eye, and arched neck convey a restless charger straining forward, yet a taut rope holds the horse beside the upright tethering post. Han Gan’s spare ink lines make that tension between movement and restraint immediately legible.
