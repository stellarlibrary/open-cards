Colossal Head of a Deva

Angkor, Cambodia, ca. 1200 • Sandstone • Cleveland Museum of Art. This monumental head belonged to a causeway balustrade depicting the Churning of the Ocean of Milk.

Look closely

The deva’s broad face is carved with smooth, calm planes and lowered eyes, whereas the tiered crown rises in tight horizontal rows of incised ornament. That contrast in surface texture separates the serene facial expression clearly from its intricate headdress.
