The Assumption of the Virgin

El Greco, 1577–79 • Oil on canvas • Art Institute of Chicago. This tall painting was made for a church altarpiece in Toledo.

Look closely

The Virgin’s bright blue robe crosses the center as she rises, while angels surround her and the apostles look and gesture upward. The lower cluster of bodies directs the eye into the bright upper space.
