Twelve-Sided Ewer with Sphinxes

Khurasan, Iran, Ilkhanid period, 1300–1350 • Hammered brass inlaid with silver • Cleveland Museum of Art.

Look closely

Silver-inlaid sphinxes and animated human-headed inscriptions repeat in horizontal registers across all twelve facets of the ewer’s body. By coordinating those bands with the angled facets, the metalworker created ornament that unfolds as the vessel is turned.
