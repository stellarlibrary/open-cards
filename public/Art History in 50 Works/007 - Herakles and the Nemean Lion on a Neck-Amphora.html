Herakles and the Nemean Lion on a Neck-Amphora

Attic Greece, 515–510 BCE • Black-figure ceramic • Painter of Berlin 1899 • Cleveland Museum of Art. The photograph shows the Herakles side.

Look closely

Thin incised lines interrupt the dark slip and define details inside the figures. That combination of dark silhouette and cut line distinguishes black-figure decoration from red-figure decoration.
