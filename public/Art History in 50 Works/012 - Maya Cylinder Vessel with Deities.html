Maya Cylinder Vessel with Deities

Chamá-style Maya, present-day Guatemala, 600–900 CE • Earthenware with colored slips • Cleveland Museum of Art.

Look closely

Horizontal bands at the rim and foot frame the robed figure on the curved wall. The viewer must turn the vessel to follow a composition that continues around it rather than sitting in a flat picture frame.
