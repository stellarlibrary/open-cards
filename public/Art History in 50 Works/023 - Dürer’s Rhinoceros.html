Dürer’s Rhinoceros

Albrecht Dürer, 1515 • Woodcut • Cleveland Museum of Art. Dürer never saw the living Indian rhinoceros brought to Lisbon in 1515, working instead from a written report and sketch.

Look closely

Sharp seams, scalloped rims, and dense scale textures divide the rhinoceros’s hide into plates resembling jointed steel armor. Multiplied through print, Dürer’s woodcut shows how a persuasive image built on secondhand testimony shaped European visual knowledge for centuries.
