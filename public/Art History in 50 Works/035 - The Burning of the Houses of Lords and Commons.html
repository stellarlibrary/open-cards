The Burning of the Houses of Lords and Commons

J. M. W. Turner, 1835 • Oil on canvas • Cleveland Museum of Art. The fire took place by the Thames in 1834.

Look closely

The river reflects the orange flames, stretching their light downward in loose strokes. The dark figures and boats near that glow help turn the blaze into a vast atmospheric event.
