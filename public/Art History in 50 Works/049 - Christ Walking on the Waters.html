Christ Walking on the Waters

Henry Ossawa Tanner, ca. 1910 • Etching • Cleveland Museum of Art. The scene is staged on dark water, not on land.

Look closely

Lines bunch densely around the tilting boat and sweep in different directions. The comparatively open water beyond it lets the rough marks around the people carry the sense of disturbance.
