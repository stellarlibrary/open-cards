Akbar Supervising the Capture of Wild Elephants

Mughal India, ca. 1602–03; mount borders added later • Pigments, ink, and gold on paper • Farukh Chela, Govardhan, and Dhanraj • Cleveland Museum of Art.

Look closely

Akbar sits on horseback toward the upper left, looking over the scene; the elephants fill the foreground. The image separates command from the action without making the emperor physically the largest figure.
