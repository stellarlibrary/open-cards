Inca Tapestry Tunic

Peru, Inca Empire, ca. 1400–1540 • Interlocked tapestry of cotton and camelid fiber • Cleveland Museum of Art. Fine state-woven tunics signaled noble rank and imperial favor.

Look closely

Dense rows of miniature diagonal-key squares—the standardized “Inca Key” pattern—fill the upper field of the tunic, while broad horizontal bands of red and dark brown anchor the lower edge. Razor-sharp color transitions across thousands of threads make the fineness of the weaving visible.
