Operating on Guan Yu’s Arm

Katsushika Ōi, Edo Japan, 1840s • Ink, color, and gold on silk • Cleveland Museum of Art. Depicts a celebrated episode from Romance of the Three Kingdoms.

Look closely

Guan Yu keeps his attention calmly on his board game while the physician cuts into his wounded arm and vividly dressed attendants recoil, turning their bodies and hands away. Katsushika Ōi makes stoic self-control legible in a single dramatic contrast.
