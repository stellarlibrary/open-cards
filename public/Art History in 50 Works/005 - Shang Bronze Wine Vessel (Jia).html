Shang Bronze Wine Vessel (Jia)

China, late Shang dynasty, ca. 1250–1046 BCE • Cast bronze • Cleveland Museum of Art. A jia was a ritual vessel for wine.

Look closely

Three tall legs lift the deep body off the surface, and two posts rise opposite one another at the rim. Together they give this type of ritual bronze a silhouette quite unlike an everyday cup.
