At the Moulin Rouge

Henri de Toulouse-Lautrec, 1892–95 • Oil on canvas • Art Institute of Chicago. A late-night scene in a Paris dance hall.

Look closely

The green-lit face is large and cropped sharply by the edge. Its cool color and proximity break into the darker, more distant group, as though someone has just passed the viewer.
