Malagan Memorial Festival Pole

New Ireland, Papua New Guinea, 1800s • Carved and painted wood with shell inlays • Cleveland Museum of Art. Revealed during malagan funerary and memorial ceremonies.

Look closely

Stacked faces, birds, and human figures interlock tightly within the tall, narrow timber, guiding the viewer’s eye upward from base to crest. Red, black, and white pigments paired with shell inlays separate intricate openwork details along the slender silhouette.
