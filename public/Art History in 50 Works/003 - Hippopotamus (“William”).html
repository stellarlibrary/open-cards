Hippopotamus (“William”)

Egypt, Middle Kingdom, ca. 1961–1878 BCE • Glazed faience • The Met. It was found in a tomb.

Look closely

Lotus blossoms and other aquatic plants make the blue hippo’s own back resemble its marsh habitat. In a tomb, lotuses also carried associations of renewal.
