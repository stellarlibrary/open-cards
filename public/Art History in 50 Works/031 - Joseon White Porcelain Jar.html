Joseon White Porcelain Jar

Korea, Joseon dynasty, 1700s • Glazed porcelain • Cleveland Museum of Art. This rounded type is often called a moon jar.

Look closely

The belly is nearly spherical but not perfectly symmetrical; it narrows into a short neck and small foot. The plain glaze leaves the contour and subtle irregularities as the main features.
