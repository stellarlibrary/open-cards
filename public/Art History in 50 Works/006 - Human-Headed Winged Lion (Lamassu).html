Human-Headed Winged Lion (Lamassu)

Neo-Assyrian palace at Nimrud, ca. 883–859 BCE • Gypsum alabaster • The Met. A guardian set at a passage.

Look closely

The sculptor gave it five legs: paired forelegs present a steady front view, while a different grouping reads as four striding legs from the side. The two views make the gateway guardian work on approach and in passing.
