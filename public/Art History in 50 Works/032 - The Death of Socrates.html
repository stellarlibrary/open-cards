The Death of Socrates

Jacques-Louis David, 1787 • Oil on canvas • The Met. The scene retells Socrates’ final moments in prison.

Look closely

He raises one hand with a finger pointing upward while the other reaches steadily toward the cup. His erect posture and controlled gestures contrast with the surrounding friends’ grief.
