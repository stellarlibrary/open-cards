Cameo: Head of Diana

Roman Italy, 25 BCE–100 CE • Carved layered glass • Cleveland Museum of Art.

Look closely

Its pale profile rises in relief against darker glass. By cutting through layers of different colors to different depths, the maker could model the face and mark out the hair without painting them on.
