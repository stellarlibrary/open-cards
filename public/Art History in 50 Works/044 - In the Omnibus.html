In the Omnibus

Mary Cassatt, 1890–91 • Color drypoint and aquatint • Cleveland Museum of Art. The scene is an ordinary ride in a public carriage.

Look closely

Clean contours set apart the passengers’ large flat areas of color, while the carriage window compresses the city into a shallow strip. The simplified view brings the riders close to the viewer.
