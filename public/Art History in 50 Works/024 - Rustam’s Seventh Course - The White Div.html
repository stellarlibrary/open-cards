Rustam’s Seventh Course: The White Div

Safavid Iran, Tabriz, ca. 1522–37 • Gum tempera, ink, and metals on paper • Cleveland Museum of Art. From a royal copy of the Shahnama.

Look closely

The fighting figures occupy a dark cave, surrounded by much brighter scalloped rocks. The sharp value contrast isolates the main action between the blocks of manuscript text.
