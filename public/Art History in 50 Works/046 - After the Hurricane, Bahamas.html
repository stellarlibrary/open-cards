After the Hurricane, Bahamas

Winslow Homer, 1899 • Watercolor over graphite • Art Institute of Chicago.

Look closely

A prone figure and broken boat occupy the beach in the foreground. The stillness of the figure and the angled wreckage give the churning water and sky a human consequence.
