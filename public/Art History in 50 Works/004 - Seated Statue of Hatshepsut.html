Seated Statue of Hatshepsut

Egypt, Dynasty 18, ca. 1473–1458 BCE • Limestone with traces of paint • The Met. Hatshepsut ruled as pharaoh.

Look closely

The striped royal headcloth and formal, frontal seated pose mark the figure as a ruler. A sculptor could present Hatshepsut in the visual language of pharaonic office without making every feature conventionally masculine.
