Utamaro: The Flirtatious Type

Kitagawa Utamaro, Edo Japan, ca. 1793 • Color woodblock print • Cleveland Museum of Art. From a series of close-up portraits.

Look closely

The turned head, extended hand, and patterned clothing fill the frame instead of receding into a setting. With the background nearly empty, small gestures and the turn of the face carry the characterization.
