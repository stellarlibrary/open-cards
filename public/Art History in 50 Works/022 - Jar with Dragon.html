Jar with Dragon

Ming China, early 1400s • Jingdezhen porcelain with cobalt under clear glaze • The Met.

Look closely

Its long body bends along the jar’s rounded wall and leads the eye around it. The painter used cobalt on the porcelain body beneath the transparent glaze, so the blue reads as part of the glossy surface.
