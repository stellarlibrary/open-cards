Mihrab (Prayer Niche)

Isfahan, Iran, 1354–55 • Mosaic of cut monochrome-glazed tiles • The Met. Built for a madrasa in Isfahan, a mihrab marks the qibla—the direction of Mecca—for prayer.

Look closely

A tall pointed arch frames the recessed prayer niche at the center of the rectangular tile wall. Intricate floral arabesques and bands of Qur’anic calligraphy in cobalt, turquoise, white, and ochre surround the arch without obscuring its directional outline.
