Safavid Royal Carpet with Silk and Metal Thread

Isfahan, Iran, 1600–1625 • Knotted silk pile and brocaded metal-wrapped thread • Cleveland Museum of Art.

Look closely

Large floral medallions repeat rhythmically down the central field of the carpet, enclosed by a continuous border of smaller blossoms and vines. Knotted silk pile enriched with shimmering metal thread makes this both an organized floor composition and a court luxury textile.
