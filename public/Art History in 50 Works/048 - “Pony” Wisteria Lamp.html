“Pony” Wisteria Lamp

Clara Wolcott Driscoll, designer; Tiffany Studios, maker, ca. 1902–10 • Leaded glass and cast bronze • Cleveland Museum of Art.

Look closely

The cast bronze base models roots, trunk, and branching openwork that climb directly into the shade, where hundreds of leaded-glass pieces hang in irregular clusters of blue and white wisteria blossoms. Structural stand and floral shade read seamlessly as one living plant.
