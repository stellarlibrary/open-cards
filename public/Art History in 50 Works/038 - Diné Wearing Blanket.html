Diné Wearing Blanket

Navajo (Diné) woman artist, ca. 1875–80 • Wool tapestry weave • Cleveland Museum of Art.

Look closely

Many narrow light-and-dark stripes establish a steady rhythm; broad red bands and dark rectangles interrupt it at intervals. The contrast reads clearly across the full blanket and is built into the weave.
