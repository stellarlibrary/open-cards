The Temple of Dendur

Roman-period Egypt, completed by 10 CE • Sandstone • The Met. The museum installation preserves a freestanding gateway and the temple proper.

Look closely

The tall pierced gateway stands apart from the lower temple with columns at its front. Passing through one threshold before reaching the next makes the approach a sequence rather than a single doorway.
