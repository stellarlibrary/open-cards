Nataraja: Shiva as Lord of Dance

Chola-period Tamil Nadu, South India, 1000s • Bronze • Cleveland Museum of Art.

Look closely

Flames surround the dancer in a ring. The small figure under his supporting foot embodies ignorance; the ring and the balanced, lifted foot place that action within Shiva’s cosmic dance.
