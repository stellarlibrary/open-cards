Kongo Power Figure (Nkisi)

Kongo-style maker, Republic of the Congo, late 1800s–early 1900s • Wood, resin, cloth, and ritual materials • Cleveland Museum of Art.

Look closely

The small carved wooden figure with its tilted head and crossed arms rises from a much bulkier rounded base packed with resin, cloth, and empowering substances. Rather than standing as a purely visual statue, the carved body worked together with those consecrated containers.
