Paris Street; Rainy Day

Gustave Caillebotte, 1877 • Oil on canvas • Art Institute of Chicago. The setting is a Paris crossing lined with recently built streets.

Look closely

The large nearby umbrella holders are cut by the edges, while the paving and building lines recede toward figures much smaller in the distance. The contrast makes the viewpoint feel like a place occupied by another pedestrian.
