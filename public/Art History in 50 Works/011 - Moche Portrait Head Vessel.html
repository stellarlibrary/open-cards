Moche Portrait Head Vessel

North coast of Peru, Moche style, ca. 200–550 CE • Earthenware with colored slips • Cleveland Museum of Art. The specific elite man portrayed is not recorded by name.

Look closely

Carefully modeled eyelids, lips, and cheekbones give this stirrup-spout vessel the individualized structure of a particular face, while slip-painted bands cross the forehead and cheeks. Rather than serving as surface decoration, the portrait head forms the body of the vessel itself.
