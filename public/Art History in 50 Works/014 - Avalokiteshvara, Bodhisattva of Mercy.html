Avalokiteshvara, Bodhisattva of Mercy

Central Java, Indonesia, 800s • Cast bronze • Cleveland Museum of Art. Avalokiteshvara embodies active Buddhist compassion.

Look closely

Avalokiteshvara sits with one leg folded across the seat while the other lowers gracefully over its edge. That balanced asymmetry softens the formality of the tall headdress and makes the bodhisattva look poised and approachable rather than rigid.
