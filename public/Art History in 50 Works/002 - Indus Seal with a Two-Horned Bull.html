Indus Seal with a Two-Horned Bull

Indus Valley civilization, ca. 2000 BCE • Carved steatite • Cleveland Museum of Art. Seals like this could be pressed into soft material.

Look closely

A bull fills the lower area; a short row of signs sits above. The animal is identifiable, but the Indus script remains undeciphered, so no exact reading of the signs is secure.
