Wheat Field with Cypresses

Vincent van Gogh, 1889 • Oil on canvas • The Met. This version belongs to the museum under accession 1993.132.

Look closely

Waves of wheat and swirls of cloud sweep sideways, while a dark upright cypress interrupts them. The upright mass gives the moving strokes a strong point of contrast.
