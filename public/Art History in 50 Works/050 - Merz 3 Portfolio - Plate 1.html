Merz 3 Portfolio: Plate 1

Kurt Schwitters, 1923 • Lithograph • Cleveland Museum of Art. Schwitters coined “Merz” for his experimental practice assembling fragments across art forms.

Look closely

Sharp-edged black rectangles, scraps of grid, and stenciled letters collide at abrupt angles and scales across the printed sheet. Although produced by lithography rather than pasted paper, the composition replaces single-point perspective with the fractured logic of collage.
