God of Thunder (Raijin)

Sōtatsu workshop, attributed, mid-1600s • Gold and pigments on a six-panel folding screen • Cleveland Museum of Art.

Look closely

The small figure and his drums are suspended high against uninterrupted gold, with long dark ribbons sweeping away. The emptiness around them makes the figure’s movement across the wide screen more conspicuous.
