Māori Neck Pendant (Hei-tiki)

Aotearoa New Zealand, 1800s • Carved pounamu (nephrite greenstone) • Cleveland Museum of Art. Hei-tiki are treasured taonga (ancestral heirlooms) passed across generations.

Look closely

The carved figure’s bent arms and legs fold inward against the torso while its large, wide-eyed head tilts sharply to one side. Compressing the body into a compact, polished contour keeps the ancestral form strong and wearable at pendant scale.
