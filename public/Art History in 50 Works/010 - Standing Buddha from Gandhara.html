Standing Buddha from Gandhara

Gandhara, present-day Pakistan, 150–200 CE • Schist • Cleveland Museum of Art.

Look closely

Curved rows of deeply carved folds sweep over the shoulders and descend over the body. The relief catches light and shadow, so the robe has weight and lies over a figure rather than looking like a flat pattern.
