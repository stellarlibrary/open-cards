Acorn Woodpecker Melanerpes formicivorus

Field marks

The pale iris stands out in a black patch around the eye, and pale patches on the forehead and throat break up the black face. The contrast is more useful than red alone.

Western North America to parts of Colombia • Oak woodland and related habitats. Some groups store acorns in trees drilled with many small holes.
