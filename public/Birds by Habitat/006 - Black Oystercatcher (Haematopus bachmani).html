Black Oystercatcher Haematopus bachmani

Field marks

A long, thick orange-red bill, orange eye ring, and pale pink legs stand out against the charcoal body. The bird uses its blade-like bill to pry loose limpets and open exposed mussels.

Pacific North America • Rocky intertidal shores, where falling tides expose mussels and limpets.

Identification note

Juveniles have a dark-tipped, duller bill and browner plumage than adults. Unlike the pied American Oystercatcher—which overlaps locally along Baja California—this rocky-shore species lacks a white belly and white wing stripe.
