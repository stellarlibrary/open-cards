Lilac-breasted Roller Coracias caudatus

Field marks

The throat and breast are lilac to pink, while the belly is turquoise-blue below a green crown and long outer tail streamers. Look for that color break on a bird waiting on an exposed branch.

Eastern and southern Africa • Open savanna and lightly wooded country, with exposed perches for hunting.

Identification note

Males and females look alike, whereas juveniles lack the long tail streamers and have a duller rufous-tawny breast instead of vivid lilac. Nonbreeding European Rollers visit the same savannas, but show an all-turquoise breast and a square tail.
