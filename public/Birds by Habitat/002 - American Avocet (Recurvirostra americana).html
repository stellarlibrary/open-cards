American Avocet Recurvirostra americana

Field marks

The tip curves upward. The avocet sweeps the fine bill from side to side through shallow water for small aquatic prey.

North American shallow wetlands • Salt ponds, alkaline lakes and tidal flats. The cinnamon head and neck are a feature of breeding plumage.
