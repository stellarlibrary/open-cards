Rainbow Lorikeet Trichoglossus moluccanus

Field marks

A short, hooked orange-red bill stands out against a blue head; the bright orange breast meets green wings and back. Flowering branches are good places to look.

Eastern Australia • Flowering trees in open woodland, towns, and coastal parks. Lorikeets feed on nectar and pollen as well as fruit.
