Black Guillemot Cepphus grylle

Field marks

Each folded wing carries a large white patch, and the feet are red. Those two bright areas stand out against the otherwise sooty breeding plumage.

North Atlantic and Arctic coasts • Shelters in rock crevices and feeds by diving in coastal water. The dark plumage shown here is seasonal.
