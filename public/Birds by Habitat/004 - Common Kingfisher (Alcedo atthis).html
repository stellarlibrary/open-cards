Common Kingfisher Alcedo atthis

Field marks

Blue-green upperparts contrast with orange below. A long, straight bill projects from its compact head; from a low perch the bird can dive bill-first for a fish.

Eurasia and North Africa • Streams, ponds, and canals with fish and low lookout perches.

Identification note

Adult males have an almost all-black bill, whereas adult females show a prominent orange-red base on the lower mandible. Because the back color is structural, its sheen can shift from emerald green to bright cobalt blue with the angle of light.
