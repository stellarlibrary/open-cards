Greater Roadrunner Geococcyx californianus

Field marks

A small, shaggy crest rises over the head, and a very long tail extends behind. The low, long-bodied silhouette helps distinguish it from smaller desert songbirds.

Southwestern United States and Mexico • Desert scrub, open brush, and rocky ground where it mostly hunts on foot.
