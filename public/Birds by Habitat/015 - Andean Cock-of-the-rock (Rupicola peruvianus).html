Andean Cock-of-the-rock Rupicola peruvianus

Field marks

A rounded, fan-like crest sweeps forward above the bill. Black wings with pale silvery-gray feathers contrast with the bright head and body.

Tropical Andes • Humid montane and cloud forests. Males can be orange or crimson; females are much duller and have smaller crests.
