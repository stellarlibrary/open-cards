Superb Fairywren Malurus cyaneus

Field marks

A narrow tail is held steeply up, and electric-blue patches mark the head against a black face and throat. Females and dull-plumaged males lack this bright pattern.

Southeastern Australia • Dense low cover, including gardens and shrubby edges. The bright blue and black are typical of a breeding male.
