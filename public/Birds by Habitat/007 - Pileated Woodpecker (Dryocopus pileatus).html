Pileated Woodpecker Dryocopus pileatus

Field marks

Look for a pointed scarlet crest above the white stripes on the dark face and neck. The male shown also has a red stripe on the cheek; the female lacks that cheek stripe.

North America • Woods and forest with standing dead wood and large trees.

Identification note

Nearly crow-sized with a solid black back, it is far larger than Downy or Hairy Woodpeckers, which have white backs and lack a pointed crest. The smaller Red-headed Woodpecker has an unstriped crimson hood covering the entire head and neck.
