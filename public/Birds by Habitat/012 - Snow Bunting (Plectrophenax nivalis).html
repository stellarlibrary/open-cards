Snow Bunting Plectrophenax nivalis

Field marks

A dark outer wing interrupts a broad white area; the head has warm buff tones. In an open winter flock, watch for the wings flashing white when the birds lift off.

Arctic tundra in the breeding season; open fields, shores, and other bare ground farther south in winter. This is a winter-plumaged bird.
