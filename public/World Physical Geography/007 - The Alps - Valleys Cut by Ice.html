The Alps: Valleys Cut by Ice

The Alps arc across south-central Europe from southeastern France through Switzerland, northern Italy, and Austria toward Slovenia, carved by deep troughs such as Switzerland's Lauterbrunnen Valley (near 46.6°N, 7.9°E).

Read the landscape

Formerly glaciated alpine valleys typically show a U-shaped cross-section rather than a narrow river-cut V: thick valley glaciers filled the trough and abraded both its walls and floor, leaving steep cliffs above a broad valley bottom after the ice retreated.
