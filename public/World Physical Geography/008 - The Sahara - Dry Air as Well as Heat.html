The Sahara: Dry Air as Well as Heat

Across North Africa between roughly 20°N and 30°N, the Sahara stretches from the Atlantic Ocean to the Red Sea, spanning rocky plateaus, gravel plains, mountain massifs, and sand seas.

Read the landscape

Latitude in the northern subtropics explains the Sahara's aridity better than temperature alone: air in the descending branch of the Hadley circulation warms and dries as it sinks, suppressing cloud growth and rainfall while heat further raises potential evaporation.
