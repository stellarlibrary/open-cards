The Amazon: From the Andes to the Atlantic

Water from Andean headwaters in Peru enters a broad lowland basin, then travels east across Brazil to the Atlantic near the equator. Major tributaries also drain large areas north and south of the main river.

Read the landscape

The basin is the whole area that drains to the river. Rainfall and runoff from many tributaries combine downstream, so the volume at the mouth reflects water collected across the lowlands as well as in the Andean headwaters.
