The Great Barrier Reef: Not a Continuous Wall

Along Queensland's northeastern continental shelf in the Coral Sea (roughly 10–25°S), the Great Barrier Reef forms a mosaic of thousands of individual reefs and shoals separated by open channels.

Read the landscape

Rather than forming one solid wall, reef-building corals grow in patches where shelf depth, warm sunlit water, and clarity align, leaving natural passages where deeper water, currents, or river sediment and freshwater plumes limit coral growth.
