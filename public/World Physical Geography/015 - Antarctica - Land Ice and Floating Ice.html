Antarctica: Land Ice and Floating Ice

Map Antarctica in a south-polar view, with the South Pole at the center and the continental margin surrounded by the Southern Ocean. Mark the grounded ice sheet inland and a floating extension such as the Ross Ice Shelf.

Read the landscape

Grounded ice adds water that was stored on land to the ocean. Floating ice already displaces seawater, though losing an ice shelf can let the grounded glaciers behind it flow into the ocean faster.
