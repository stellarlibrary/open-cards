The Canadian Shield: Old Rocks, Young Lakes

The Canadian Shield is a broad area of ancient bedrock around Hudson Bay, near 58°N, 85°W. On a map, look for the lake-rich parts of northern Ontario and Québec south and east of the bay.

Read the landscape

During the last ice ages, moving ice scoured the bedrock and glacial deposits disrupted drainage. When the ice retreated, many of the depressions filled with water, so the lakes are far younger than the underlying rocks.
