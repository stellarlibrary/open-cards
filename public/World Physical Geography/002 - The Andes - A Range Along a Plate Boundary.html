The Andes: A Range Along a Plate Boundary

The Andes run north–south along western South America from Venezuela and Colombia to Chile and Argentina. Near 20°S, the deep Peru–Chile Trench lies just offshore to the west of the coastal margin and high mountains inland.

Read the landscape

One tectonic process links both features: the oceanic Nazca Plate subducts eastward beneath the South American Plate. Downward flexing of the oceanic slab forms the offshore trench, while plate compression thickens and uplifts continental crust and flux melting feeds volcanic segments.
