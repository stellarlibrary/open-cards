The Nile: Two Rivers Meet at Khartoum

The White Nile flows generally north into Sudan, and the Blue Nile flows from the Ethiopian Highlands into Sudan. They meet at Khartoum, near 16°N, 33°E; the combined river heads north through Egypt.

Read the landscape

Rain over the Ethiopian Highlands increases Blue Nile runoff, adding to the combined flow at Khartoum. This helped drive the historic summer flood in Egypt before extensive dam regulation changed the downstream flow.
