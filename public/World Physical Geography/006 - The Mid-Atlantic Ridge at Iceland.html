The Mid-Atlantic Ridge at Iceland

The Mid-Atlantic Ridge runs along the Atlantic floor between the Americas on the west and Europe and Africa on the east, rising above sea level across Iceland near 65°N, 19°W.

Read the landscape

Seafloor spreading explains why crustal age increases symmetrically away from the ridge: magma solidifies into young basaltic crust along the rift axis as the North American and Eurasian plates diverge, carrying older rock progressively farther west and east.
