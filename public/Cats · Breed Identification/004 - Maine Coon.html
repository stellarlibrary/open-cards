Maine Coon

A large, rectangular cat with a shaggy coat, a long tail and a notably square muzzle.

Look for

Look at the high ears, shaggy neck ruff and full tail. From the front, the muzzle is typically square; that cue is subtler in a three-quarter view.

Tell it apart

The Norwegian Forest Cat has a more triangular head and straighter nose profile. Long hair or ear tufts alone do not distinguish them.
