Birman

A semi-longhaired colorpoint cat with blue eyes and white feet.

Look for

The dark mask and blue eyes resemble a Siamese, but the cheeks are fuller and the white gloves stand out against the darker legs.

Tell it apart

A mitted Ragdoll also has white front paws, along with a white chin, whereas Birmans keep a dark chin and show pointed white wedges ('laces') up the backs of the hind feet; one front view may not distinguish them.
