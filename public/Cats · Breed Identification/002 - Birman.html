Birman

A semi-longhaired colourpoint cat with blue eyes and white feet.

Look for

The dark mask and blue eyes resemble a Siamese, but the cheeks are fuller and the white gloves stand out against the darker legs.

Tell it apart

A mitted Ragdoll can also have white feet. Birmans typically have white wedges extending up the backs of the hind feet; one front view may not distinguish them.
