British Shorthair

A sturdy, rounded cat with a short, plush coat and a broad face.

Look for

Look for full cheeks, widely set rounded ears and a compact body. This blue example illustrates one of many possible colours.

Tell it apart

A Russian Blue usually has a more slender frame, a wedge-shaped head and green eyes. Blue-grey fur alone is not a reliable guide.
