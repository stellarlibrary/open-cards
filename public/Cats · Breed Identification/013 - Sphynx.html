Sphynx

A cat with very little visible fur; fine down may remain. Prominent ears and skin folds help form its silhouette.

Look for

Look for large open ears, soft folds around the head or shoulders, and a long, thin tail. Skin color can show pattern without a visible hair coat.

Tell it apart

A Devon Rex also has large ears but usually has a soft, clearly wavy coat rather than the Sphynx appearance.
