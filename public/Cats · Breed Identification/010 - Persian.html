Persian

A round-headed, short-muzzled cat with long fur and a full ruff.

Look for

Look for small ears, a broad face and thick fur extending around the face and down the body. Facial profiles vary among Persians.

Tell it apart

An Exotic Shorthair has a similar rounded head and short nose but a much shorter, dense coat.
