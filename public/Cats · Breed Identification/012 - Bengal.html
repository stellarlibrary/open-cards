Bengal

A muscular shorthaired cat with a highly patterned coat. Spotted cats often have two-tone markings called rosettes; marbled Bengals also occur.

Look for

Look for individual spots with contrasting centres on this cat’s flank. The body is longer and more athletic-looking than the pattern alone might suggest.

Tell it apart

A classic tabby may have swirls, and other breeds can be spotted. Breed identification needs the combination of shape, coat and pattern.
