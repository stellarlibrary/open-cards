Chartreux

A sturdy blue-grey cat with a dense coat and copper-to-gold adult eyes.

Look for

Look for gold eyes and a muzzle that narrows below the cheeks. The legs are relatively fine under a sturdy torso.

Tell it apart

A blue British Shorthair often has a rounder head and thicker-looking legs. A Russian Blue has a slimmer body and green adult eyes.
