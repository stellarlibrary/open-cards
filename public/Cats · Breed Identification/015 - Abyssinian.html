Abyssinian

A lithe cat with upright ears and a ticked coat: individual hairs carry alternating colour bands.

Look for

The body coat has a warm, finely shaded appearance rather than bold flank stripes or Bengal-like rosettes. The individual hair bands are easier to see close up.

Tell it apart

Colour varies. Compare the evenly distributed ticking with the discrete spots or marbling of a Bengal.
