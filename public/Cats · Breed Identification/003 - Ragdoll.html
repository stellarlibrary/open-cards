Ragdoll

A large, long-bodied, semi-longhaired colourpoint cat with blue eyes. White markings vary by pattern.

Look for

The coat gives a soft outline around the body and tail. In the bicolour pattern, an inverted white V on the face can divide the darker mask.

Tell it apart

Birmans typically retain a dark face with white feet. A mitted Ragdoll may look more similar; check the chin, hind feet and overall build together.
