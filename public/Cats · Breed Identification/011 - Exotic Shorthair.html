Exotic Shorthair

A compact, round-headed cat with a plush coat shorter than a Persian’s.

Look for

The eyes are set in a broad face with a short nose. The dense, standing coat looks plush rather than forming the long flowing ruff of a Persian.

Tell it apart

A British Shorthair also looks rounded and plush, but the Exotic typically has a much shorter profile with a more pronounced stop between brow and nose.
