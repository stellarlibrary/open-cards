Devon Rex

A short-coated cat with fine waves, a short face and very large low-set ears.

Look for

Check the wide ear bases and cheekbones before looking closely at the coat texture. The hair is soft and wavy and can be sparse in parts.

Tell it apart

A Cornish Rex is also wavy, but usually has a longer egg-shaped head and higher-set ears.
